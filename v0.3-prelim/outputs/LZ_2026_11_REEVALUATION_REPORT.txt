2. WHAT THE PAPER ACTUALLY IS
=====================================================================
Primary hit: arXiv:2609.08893v1 (hep-ph, 2026-09-08, 7 pages)
  Yuan, Zhang, Cao, Feng & Yang, "ALP-mediated inelastic dark matter and
  the LUX-ZEPLIN high-recoil candidate event LZ230616"
  Local PDF: docs/papers/verified/2609.08893_alp_lz230616_2026.pdf
  KB summary: docs/papers/verified/2609.08893_alp_lz230616_2026.md

Content (read end-to-end from the PDF text, not the abstract): two
Majorana dark-matter states split by delta, scattering endothermically
(chi1 N -> chi2 N) off xenon through an axion-like particle coupled to
gluons. Leading nucleon operator O6 with momentum-dependent pion/eta
nucleon form factors, full WimPyDD nuclear responses, SHM halo
(v0 = 238 km/s, v_esc = 544 km/s, rho = 0.3 GeV/cm^3), LZ exposure
2.84 tonne-years, window 5.4-269.9 keV, energy kernel 32.53 keV.

Table I benchmarks (m_a = 0.3 GeV), read from the PDF:

  benchmark            m_chi [TeV]  delta [keV]  q_LZ  |g_G| [GeV^-2]
  (a) best spectrum       0.35          330        0      0.21
  (b) q_LZ <= 1           1.25          320      0.99     2.30e-4
  (c) q_LZ <= 2           1.04          225      1.99     4.30e-5
  (d) elastic axion       5.0             0       2.84     1.42e-5

The fit statistic is an approximate single-event recoil-energy likelihood
and the paper says of itself that it "neither reproduces the official LZ
likelihood nor defines statistically calibrated confidence regions", and
that the quoted couplings "do not include a relic-abundance prior or
collider and flavor constraints".

=====================================================================
3. SIGMA_DM-NUCLEON LIMIT: NONE COMPARABLE EXISTS
=====================================================================
Mechanical scan of the paper's full text: 0 occurrences of "cm-2",
"cm^2", "exclusion", "upper limit", "90% CL", "95% CL". There is no
sigma_SI or sigma_DM-nucleon number anywhere in it, at any mass, at any
confidence level.

Why: the paper's output is the coupling product |g_G| REQUIRED to produce
one expected event under a conditional signal hypothesis - the opposite
kind of statement from an exclusion limit. It also does not derive
significance; its "3.4 local / 2.6 global" is quoted from the LZ paper
(arXiv:2609.02823).

Companion hits, triaged:
  - 2610.32747v1 (Aristizabal Sierra+): sets real 90% CL limits, but on
    neutrino-fermion up-scattering couplings, not sigma_DM-nucleon;
    concludes up-scattering does not explain the event.
  - 2609.05291v1 (Gu+): inelastic xenon-excitation companion channel,
    rate O(0.1) of elastic or below - a future-search study, no limit
    relevant here.
  - 2609.04185v2 (Jeesun & Majumdar+): atmospheric-neutrino up-scattering
    interpretation; an allowed parameter region, not a DM-nucleon limit.

The LZ collaboration's own paper (2609.02823) does set new limits -
two-sided 90% CL intervals on NREFT operator couplings (its Fig 6, called
world-leading for those operators) and a recast of the inelastic scalar
operator O1s to a DM-nucleon cross section with scalar normalization
(Fig S7, at a 1000 GeV WIMP). But these constrain different observables:
inelastic two-state operators with mass splitting, and dimensionless EFT
couplings - not the elastic spin-independent sigma_DM-nucleon that the
project's Channel 5 maps onto.
