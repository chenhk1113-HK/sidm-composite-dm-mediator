## Why T134 v2 was wrong

1. **Circular reasoning**: The data in `sigma_m_phase44.json` was
   generated by `joint_fit_full_evaluation(a_slope_override=1.0)`,
   which uses `yukawa_bg(v, sigma_0, a_slope=1.0)` = exactly v^(-1).
   So data has slope = -1.0 by construction.

2. **Misread CFT 2021**: The paper explicitly restricts α to [0.5, 1.0]
   for calculability/unitarity:
   > "Bulk mass: 1/2 ≤ α ≤ 1 (Calculability, unitarity)"
   > "we further restrict the range to α ≥ 1/2 to avoid the regime
   > where the long-range potential is steeper than the centrifugal term"

   α = 0.25 (which we claimed matched) is **outside this valid range**.

## Honest assessment of CFT 2021

CFT 2021 is a real, peer-reviewed framework (arXiv:2102.05674, JHEP 06
(2021) 008, 34 citations). For α ∈ [0.5, 1.0]:

| α | σ_T scaling | Physics |
|---|---|---|
| 0.5 | v^(-2) | Standard Born (boundary case) |
| 0.55 | v^(-2.2) | Example case in CFT 2021 |
| 1.0 | 4D Yukawa | Single mediator (boundary) |

For α = 0.25 (which we'd need for our slope = -1): NOT in valid range.

## Correct CFT 2021 verdict

**CFT 2021 does NOT match our data** for α ∈ [0.5, 1.0]. It would only
match if our slope were in [-2.0, -4.0], but our slope is ≈ -1.0.
