## The three unknowns being probed

At the end of the Phase 11-14 series, three unknown questions remained:

1. **Can g_D exceed 0.16 via a different annihilation channel?**
   (Phase 15)
2. **Does asymmetric DM at m_chi = 45 GeV give a natural η_DM/η_B?**
   (Phase 16)
3. **Do core-collapsed halos prefer a different g_D than the bulk?**
   (Phase 17)

## Phase 15 — g_D Limit per Annihilation Channel

### Question

The Fermi dwarf limit (σv < 5×10⁻²⁶ cm³/s for bb̄) forces g_D ≤ 0.16 in our pipeline. If Majorana χ annihilates through a different final state (ττ, μμ, e+e-, light quarks, WW, ZZ, hh, νν), does the limit relax enough to allow g_D ~ 0.5-0.7 (the SIDM-required value)?

### Method

Compute σv for χχ → XX at v_rel ~ 10⁻³ c (freeze-out regime) for each
SM final state. Compare to channel-by-channel Fermi upper limit. Find
the maximum g_D that satisfies the limit per channel.

### Results — 14 channels tested

| Channel | Fermi limit (cm³/s) | g_D_max | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| e+e- | 3×10⁻²⁶ | **0.151** | CONSTRAINED |
| μ+μ- | 5×10⁻²⁶ | 0.172 | CONSTRAINED |
| τ+τ- | 5×10⁻²⁶ | 0.172 | CONSTRAINED |
| bb̄ | 5×10⁻²⁶ | 0.172 | CONSTRAINED |
| uu, dd, ss, cc | 1×10⁻²⁵ | 0.204 | CONSTRAINED |
| gg | 1×10⁻²⁵ | 0.204 | CONSTRAINED |
| νν | 1×10⁻²⁵ | 0.204 | CONSTRAINED |
| **tt** | 1×10⁻²³ | **5.000** | **OK** (but forbidden) |
| **WW** | 1×10⁻²⁵ | 5.000 | OK (forbidden) |
| **ZZ** | 1×10⁻²⁵ | 5.000 | OK (forbidden) |
| **hh** | 1×10⁻²⁵ | 5.000 | OK (forbidden) |

### Verdict

**The Fermi limit is STRUCTURAL.** All kinematically accessible channels
constrain g_D to **0.15-0.21**. Heavier channels (tt, WW, ZZ, hh) would
allow larger g_D but are kinematically forbidden at m_chi = 45 GeV.

There is no SM final state that can relieve the g_D ~ 0.16 limit without:
- Lowering m_chi to make tt, WW, etc. accessible
- Adding a non-SM final state (e.g., χχ → A'A' if m_A' < m_chi)
- Going to a different dark matter model entirely

## Phase 16 — Asymmetric DM Baryon-to-DM Ratio

### Question

If Majorana χ is the matter-asymmetric component (Phase 11), the DM
density η_DM should track the baryon asymmetry η_B through some common
generation mechanism. Does the v0.3-prelim Majorana reframe at m_chi = 45 GeV
predict a natural η_DM/η_B ratio?

### Method

In asymmetric DM with a common B-L-generating mechanism, the relic
abundance is:
  η_DM / η_B = (Ω_DM × m_p) / (Ω_B × m_chi)

For 1:1 transfer (single mechanism), this gives m_chi = (Ω_DM × m_p) / Ω_B
= 5.04 GeV.

### Results

| m_chi [GeV] | η_DM/η_B | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 5.04 | UNNATURAL (extreme) |
| **5** | **1.008** | **NATURAL (1:1 transfer)** |
| 10 | 0.504 | NATURAL |
| 15 | 0.336 | PLAUSIBLE |
| **45** | **0.112** | **PLAUSIBLE but TUNED (8.9×)** |
| 100 | 0.050 | TUNED |
| 500 | 0.010 | UNNATURAL |
| 1000 | 0.005 | UNNATURAL |

### Verdict

**v0.3-prelim's m_chi = 45 GeV is TUNED for asymmetric DM.**

For 1:1 B-L transfer, the natural mass is **m_chi ~ 5 GeV** (Kaplan+ 2009
expectation). At m_chi = 45 GeV, the asymmetry mechanism must transfer
**8.9× more asymmetry to χ than to baryons**. This is PLAUSIBLE (the
mechanism is model-dependent and not necessarily 1:1) but TUNED.

**Implication**: If the v0.3-prelim Majorana reframe is correct, the
asymmetry-generation mechanism is NOT a simple 1:1 B-L transfer.
Possibilities:
- Multi-step transfer with intermediate states
- Asymmetry that depends on m_chi (e.g., Affleck-Dine with χ-specific
  flat direction)
- Coincidence: m_chi = 45 GeV just happens to give the right asymmetry

## Phase 17 — Core-Collapse Time vs g_D

### Question

Core-collapsed halos (observed in some dwarf galaxies via strong lensing
and stellar kinematics) require a faster gravothermal collapse than the
current g_D = 0.16 (Fermi limit) provides. Does the same Majorana χ
produce both stable AND collapsed halos at the same g_D?

### Method

Compute τ_core (gravothermal collapse time) as a function of g_D using
the Balberg+ 2002 / Koda+ 2011 scaling:
  τ_core ~ 10 Gyr × (0.1 cm²/g / σ/m)

For typical MW-like halos, τ ~ 10 Gyr at σ/m = 0.1 cm²/g.

### Results

| g_D | σ/m(v=100) | τ_core [Gyr] | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.05 | 0.00000 | 25000 | TOO SLOW |
| 0.10 | 0.00004 | 25000 | TOO SLOW |
| 0.16 | 0.0002 | **6260** | **TOO SLOW** |
| 0.20 | 0.0004 | 2564 | TOO SLOW |
| 0.30 | 0.0020 | 506 | TOO SLOW |
| 0.50 | 0.0152 | 65.6 | TOO SLOW |
| **0.70** | 0.0585 | **17.1** | **MATCH (10 Gyr)** |
| **0.80** | 0.1000 | **10.0** | **EXACT MATCH** |
| 1.00 | 0.2437 | 4.1 | TOO FAST |

### Verdict

**Core-collapsed halos PREFER g_D ~ 0.8, which is 5× the Fermi limit.**

At g_D = 0.16 (Fermi limit), τ_core = 6260 Gyr — far longer than the
age of the universe. No collapse is possible.

For τ = 10 Gyr (observed collapsed halos), g_D = 0.80 is required. This
gives σ/m = 0.1 cm²/g, matching the cross-section of core-collapsed
halos observed in nature.

### Implications

This is a **genuine inconsistency** in the Majorana reframe at the
Fermi-limited g_D = 0.16:

1. **Bulk SIDM halos** (dwarfs, LSBs, MW) require σ/m ~ 1 cm²/g at v=100
   → need g_D ~ 0.7
2. **Core-collapsed halos** require σ/m ~ 0.1 cm²/g with τ ~ 10 Gyr
   → need g_D ~ 0.8
3. **Fermi dwarf limit** forces g_D ≤ 0.16
4. **Asymmetric DM at m_chi = 45 GeV** requires the B-L mechanism to
   transfer 8.9× more asymmetry to χ than to baryons (TUNED)

**The Majorana reframe is internally INCONSISTENT at m_chi = 45 GeV**:
- g_D = 0.16 satisfies Fermi but cannot explain SIDM (σ/m too low)
- g_D = 0.7-0.8 explains SIDM but violates Fermi

Three possible resolutions:
1. **g_D varies by halo** (e.g., density-dependent g_D via some new
   physics) — but no obvious mechanism
2. **Multi-component χ**: heavy χ has g_D ~ 0.16 (Fermi OK), light χ
   has g_D ~ 0.7 (SIDM OK), gravothermal separation explains the
   difference
3. **A different dark matter model entirely** (e.g., SIMP, ELDER,
   freeze-in) where the Fermi limit doesn't apply

## Combined Phase 15-17 verdict

| Probe | Result |
|---|---|
| 15: g_D limit per channel | STRUCTURAL — no SM channel can relieve |
| 16: η_DM/η_B at 45 GeV | TUNED — natural mass is 5 GeV |
| 17: core collapse vs g_D | INCONSISTENT — collapsed halos need g_D ~ 0.8 (5× Fermi limit) |

### Key takeaway

The Majorana reframe at m_chi = 45 GeV with g_D ~ 0.7 (SIDM-required) is
**structurally inconsistent with the Fermi dwarf limit**. This is the
first real "KILL" verdict for the Majorana reframe at the v0.3-prelim
parameter point.

**Three possible ways forward**:
- (a) Drop m_chi to ~5 GeV (asymmetric DM natural mass scale)
- (b) Drop g_D to ~0.16 (Fermi limit) and abandon SIDM
- (c) Add new physics (multi-component χ, density-dependent g_D, etc.)

The simplest resolution is (a): m_chi = 5 GeV is the natural asymmetric
DM mass AND the natural SIDM mass for galactic cores (Tulin-Yu-Zurek
2013). This would require re-running the entire v0.3-prelim pipeline
with m_chi as a free parameter near 5 GeV.
