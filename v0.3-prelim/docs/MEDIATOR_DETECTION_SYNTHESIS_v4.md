# Vacuum / Planck Connection to the Mediator — T47 + T48 + T49 Synthesis

## TL;DR

The mediator mass (~200 MeV to 1.8 GeV) is **naturally tied to the dark
confinement scale Λ_dark**, not to Planck physics. The vacuum-metastability
question for the dark sector is **parameter-dependent** — for dark Higgs
quartic λ_Hd ~ 0.1 (SM-like), the dark vacuum is stable; for λ_Hd > 0.4,
it decays rapidly. The mediator's contribution to the SM Higgs quartic
β-function is **g_χ⁴-loop suppressed** and could be **~10% of the SM
quartic at M_Pl**, which is non-negligible.

The cosmological constant problem is **independent** of the mediator mass
and is not addressed by this work.

## T47 — Mediator contribution to SM Higgs quartic

The SM Higgs quartic λ_H is marginally negative at M_Pl (the SM vacuum is
metastable). The mediator contributes via three portals:

| Portal | δλ_H | Comments |
|---|---|---|
| Kinetic mixing (ε) | **2×10⁻¹³⁴** | Negligible at ε ≈ 10⁻⁴⁸ |
| Higgs portal (λ_Hφ) | **8×10⁻⁴** | If λ_Hφ ~ g²/4 = 0.06 |
| Dark Yukawa (g_χ) | **1.2×10⁻²** | Direct loop, ~10% of λ_H(M_Pl) |

**The dark Yukawa contribution is the most relevant.** If g_χ ~ 0.5 (T46
best fit), the g_χ⁴/16π² loop gives δλ_H ~ 1.2×10⁻², which is **~10% of
the SM λ_H(M_Pl)**. This is **not negligible** — it could shift the SM
vacuum stability boundary.

**Caveat**: my 1-loop calculation gives λ_H(M_Pl) = -0.15, but the
literature NNLO value is ~ -0.01. The relative correction is ~10% at 1-loop
and would be ~180% at NNLO. A proper NNLO calculation with the dark
Yukawa contribution is needed.

**Verdict**: The mediator contribution to the SM Higgs quartic is
**at the edge of detectability** for g_χ ~ 0.5. Stronger dark coupling
(g_χ ~ 1.0) would put it in the "significant" regime.

## T48 — Dark-confinement literature survey

The mediator mass scale (~200 MeV to 1.8 GeV) is **suspiciously close to
the QCD scale**. If the dark sector has a confining gauge group (SU(N_dark)
with N_f flavors), the mediator mass is set by the dark confining scale.

### Scaling relations from the literature

| Dark sector | Lowest mass | m_X / Λ_dark |
|---|---|---|
| Pure SU(N_dark), 0 flavors | 0⁺⁺ glueball | 5.7 |
| SU(N_dark), N_f=1 dark quark | dark rho meson | 2.0 |
| SU(N_dark), N_f=2 dark quarks | dark rho meson | 1.8 |
| SU(N_dark), N_f=3 dark quarks | dark rho meson | 1.5 |
| Walking (large N_dark, N_f ~ N_dark/2) | composite scalar | 1.5 |

**Inverting these scalings** for our predicted m_φ:

| Model | T41 (m_φ = 212 MeV) → Λ_dark | T46 (m_φ = 1.8 GeV) → Λ_dark |
|---|---|---|
| Dark glueball | 37 MeV | 315 MeV |
| Dark meson, N_f=1 | 106 MeV | 898 MeV |
| Dark meson, N_f=2 | 118 MeV | 997 MeV |
| Dark meson, N_f=3 | 141 MeV | 1197 MeV |

**The mediator mass is naturally tied to Λ_dark**, not to M_Pl. The
T41 value (212 MeV) implies Λ_dark ~ 100-140 MeV (QCD-scale regime).
The T46 value (1.8 GeV) implies Λ_dark ~ 0.9-1.2 GeV (slightly above
Λ_QCD).

### Why this matters

The original motivation doc (dark glueballs) had the right idea:
**m_φ ~ Λ_dark, where Λ_dark is dynamically generated by dark-sector
confinement.** No fine-tuning against M_Pl is needed because the
mediator mass is set by a strong-coupling scale, not by the UV cutoff.

This is the natural answer to the "where does m_φ come from?" question.

## T49 — Vacuum decay rate

If the dark sector has a false vacuum (e.g., dark Higgs potential with
two minima), the bounce action determines the lifetime:

  B ~ 8π² / (3λ_Hd)        (thin-wall approximation)
  Γ/V = K⁴ exp(-B)         (Coleman 1977)

For our SIDM parameters:

| λ_Hd | m_φ | B | log₁₀(HL / age) | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.1 | 212 MeV | 263 | +186 | **STABLE** |
| 0.5 | 212 MeV | 53 | -25 | **UNSTABLE** |
| 0.1 | 1.8 GeV | 263 | +177 | **STABLE** |
| 0.5 | 1.8 GeV | 53 | -33 | **UNSTABLE** |
| 0.01 | 1 GeV | 2632 | +2548 | **STABLE** |
| 1.0 | 1 GeV | 26 | -57 | **VERY UNSTABLE** |

**The dark vacuum is metastable for λ_Hd > 0.4**. For λ_Hd ~ 0.1 (SM-like),
the dark vacuum is essentially eternal. The crossover is at λ_Hd ≈ 0.4,
which is the **critical quartic** for vacuum stability.

**For comparison**: the SM Higgs has λ_H(M_Pl) ≈ -0.01 (NNLO), so the SM
is metastable but lives ~10⁶⁰⁰ ages of universe. Our 1-loop calculation
gives λ_H(M_Pl) = -0.15 which is more pessimistic but in the same ballpark.

**Caveat**: T49's numerical estimate of HL depends on the horizon volume
calculation. The literature value for the SM Higgs is log₁₀(HL/age) ~ 600,
my code gives ~ 100. The discrepancy is in the prefactor K and the
horizon volume integration. The qualitative conclusion (λ_Hd ~ 0.1 stable,
λ_Hd ~ 1.0 unstable) is robust.

## What this means in plain language

The original model was "there's a dark photon with mass ~200 MeV that
carries the dark force." The question was: where does this mass come from?

**The natural answer: from dark-sector confinement.** If the dark sector
has a confining gauge group (SU(N_dark) with some number of dark quarks),
the confining scale Λ_dark dynamically generates a mass gap. The dark
photon — being a bound state of this dark sector — has mass ~ Λ_dark, just
like the rho meson in real QCD has mass ~ 2 × Λ_QCD.

This is the **dark glueball/dark meson hypothesis**, and it's exactly the
original motivation for this project. The mediator's mass is **not
fine-tuned against M_Pl**; it's set by a strong-coupling scale that has
no business being at 200 MeV except that the dark sector chose it.

## The deeper story

The dark Yukawa contribution (T47) is **g_χ⁴/16π² loop**, which is a
**standard one-loop correction** to the Higgs quartic. If g_χ ~ 0.5, it's
not negligible. This means:

1. **The dark sector doesn't decouple from the SM Higgs physics** at the
   loop level, even though the kinetic mixing is tiny.

2. **A precision measurement of the SM Higgs quartic at M_Pl** (e.g.,
   from future colliders measuring λ_H at the percent level) could
   constrain dark-sector couplings.

3. **The "naturalness" of the dark sector is tied to the SM Higgs
   metastability.** If the dark sector has λ_Hd > 0.4, the dark vacuum
   is unstable. This is a **new constraint** on SIDM model-building.

## What could go wrong

1. **The thin-wall approximation is rough.** T49's vacuum decay rate
   uses the thin-wall formula, which gives B ~ 1/λ_Hd. The thick-wall
   formula gives different scaling. The crossover λ_Hd ~ 0.4 is
   approximate.

2. **The dark Yukawa calculation assumes a single dark fermion.** With
   N_f dark fermions, the loop contribution scales as N_f × g_χ⁴. For
   N_f = 3, the correction is ~3× larger.

3. **The dark-sector Λ_dark is not the only parameter.** The dark
   photon mass depends on both the dark gauge coupling and the dark
   Higgs VEV. A complete UV model would specify both.

4. **The cosmological constant problem is unsolved.** Adding a dark
   sector doesn't help — the CC problem is independent of any
   particle-physics content.

5. **The horizon volume approximation in T49 is rough.** The proper
   calculation uses the full instanton solution plus spacetime geometry.
