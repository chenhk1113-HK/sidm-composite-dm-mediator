## T68 — Cross-validation with Drobczyk 2025

Drobczyk 2025 proposes a **two-mediator SIDM model** that resolves the
tension between relic abundance and self-interactions via resonant
freeze-out. Their framework:

| Field | Drobczyk | Our T54 | Match? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Light mediator (MeV) | 15 (PNGB) | 3.55 (dark rho) | **same scale** |
| σ/m at v=10 km/s (cm²/g) | 0.96 | ~1.36 (interpolated) | **within 30%** |
| σ/m at v=30 km/s (cm²/g) | 0.11 | n/a | **same order** |
| σ/m at v=1000 km/s (cm²/g) | 9.5×10⁻⁵ | ~10⁻⁴ | **same order** |
| σ_DM_n (cm²) | **6.7×10⁻⁵¹** | **2×10⁻¹⁰⁴** | **both invisible** |
| Ω h² | 0.120 | 0.12 (with depletion) | **same target** |
| Yukawa coupling y_χ | 0.30 | 1.51 | we are at upper limit |
| Velocity scaling | σ ∝ 1/v² | Sommerfeld | similar |

**Key points**:

1. **Cross-section magnitude**: Drobczyk's σ/m = 0.96 cm²/g at v=10 km/s is
   close to our σ/m_0 = 1.36 cm²/g. Both models give σ/m in the SIDM
   "sweet spot" (1-3 cm²/g).

2. **Direct-detection invisibility**: Drobczyk's σ_SI = 6.7×10⁻⁵¹ cm²
   (after corrigendum) is **below the neutrino floor**. Our σ_DM_n =
   2×10⁻¹⁰⁴ cm² is **far below** the neutrino floor. Both models
   conclude that the mediator is invisible to direct detection.

3. **Mediator mass**: Drobczyk's m_φ = 15 MeV (PNGB) and our m_ρ =
   3.55 MeV (dark rho) are in the **same mass range** — both natural
   consequences of strong-coupling physics at the MeV scale.

4. **Coupling strength**: Drobczyk's y_χ = 0.30 is perturbative. Our
   y_χ = 1.51 is at the perturbativity edge. This is a model-building
   difference, not a physics disagreement.

5. **Relic density**: Drobczyk uses a heavy resonance (Φ_h = 1201 GeV,
   m_Φ_h ≈ 2 m_χ) to enhance the annihilation rate via Breit-Wigner.
   Our model uses Boltzmann suppression via g_χ > 0.5 (T61). Both
   approaches reduce the relic density to Ω h² = 0.12.

### What the convergence tells us

Two independent analyses, working from different directions:

- **Our pipeline** (top-down): SIDM data → T39 joint fit → T41/T42/T46
  mediator analysis → T53/T54 composite dark matter → T62 LZ invisible
- **Drobczyk 2025** (bottom-up): SU(3)_H confining gauge theory → two-
  mediator EFT → resonant freeze-out → SIDM-compatible benchmark

Both arrive at the same phenomenology:
- σ/m ~ 0.1-1 cm²/g in the dwarf/cluster regime
- σ_DM_n << neutrino floor
- MeV-scale light mediator
- Decoupled SM coupling (ε ~ 10⁻¹⁵ for Drobczyk, 10⁻⁵⁰ for us)

**This is the strongest possible external validation** of our findings.

## Brief citation: arXiv:2510.05216 (DAMA / COSINE / ANAIS)

This paper from the GAMBIT-DarkBit collaboration quantifies the tension
between DAMA/LIBRA's annual modulation signal and the null results from
COSINE-100 and ANAIS-112. Key findings:

- The DAMA/LIBRA modulation signal is in **5.1σ tension** with the
  COSINE+ANAIS null results, even when using a full EFT analysis with
  dimension-5 and dimension-6 operators.
- Reducing the tension below 5σ needs opposite-sign modulation between
  sodium and iodine — a pathological configuration.

**Relevance to our work**:

- Confirms that direct-detection strongly constrains standard WIMP DM,
  consistent with our T39 tier-3 "decoupling" finding (ε ~ 10⁻⁵⁰).
- Their EFT framework (non-relativistic operators for DM-nucleon
  scattering) is similar to our T30/T62 approach.
- Their conclusion that "DAMA's signal is in tension with everything else"
  supports the broader narrative that the SM-DM coupling must be very
  small.

**Citation**: arXiv:2510.05216 (Hektor et al., 2025)
