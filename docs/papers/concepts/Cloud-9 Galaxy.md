# Cloud-9 Galaxy

A **Reionization-Limited HI Cloud (RELHIC)** near M94, discovered by Anand+ 2025 ([[2508.20157 Anand+ 2025 (Cloud-9)]]).

## Canonical v19.2-F values
- **W50 (HI line width):** 12 km/s
- **σ_v (1D line-of-sight velocity dispersion):** 5.096 km/s (= W50/2.355)
- **H I mass:** 10⁶ M☉
- **M_halo:** 5×10⁹ M☉
- **Distance:** 4.4 Mpc (M94 distance)
- **v_recession:** 304 km/s
- **NFW v_max:** ~10 km/s

## Why it's the v19.2-F target
The v19.2-F project targets σ/m = 174 cm²/g at v = 29.4 km/s. This peak velocity comes from the Cloud-9 galaxy's velocity scale (the 29.4 km/s value, plus 4.4 km/s width, gives a Gaussian peak).

## v_resonance formula
m_ρ = (v_res × μ_red) / c = (30 × 0.5) / 3×10⁵ = **0.05 MeV** (the project's adopted mediator mass)
