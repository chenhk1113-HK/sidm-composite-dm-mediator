## Key Findings

1. **Phenomenology addressed:** the dark-matter **deficit in massive systems** (stellar-kinematic and HI inner-fraction tensions of the Lei/Wang type) as a function of halo mass.
2. **Model:** exothermic inelastic dark matter 2→1 with mass splitting **Δ = 18.1 keV**, threshold velocity **v_k = 513 km/s**, peak cross-section **σ(300 km/s)/m = 0.949 cm²/g** (elastic + inelastic channels; down-scattering enhanced, low-velocity rate suppressed by K(30)/K(300) = 0.0114).
3. **Mechanism:** in massive/cluster halos (v ≳ v_k) up-scattering converts 2-component DM into the 1-component, hotter species → less bound DM in inner regions; inverse channel opens at 2v_k = 1026 km/s, placing the threshold naturally at cluster velocities.
4. **Dwarf regime:** at 30 km/s the model is nearly elastic and can sit inside the dwarf-favored band (σ ~ 0.1-10 cm²/g gray region from the dwarf-clustering observation cited as Ref. [31]).
