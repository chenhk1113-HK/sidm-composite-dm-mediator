## Key Findings

1. **Method:** orbital integration (galpy) of Euclid ERO globular-cluster candidates in 16 dwarfs of the Fornax cluster and 826 dwarfs of Perseus, using FDM soliton+halo profiles and FDM-modified dynamical friction (Szpilfidel+ 2026 force class); mass parameter m22 = m/(10⁻²² eV) scanned over 0.1–100.
2. **Result:** GCs stop spiraling into dwarf centers when **m22 ≤ 1.46 (+0.35/−0.21)** (Fornax cluster) and **m22 ≤ 1.09 (+0.12/−0.11)** (Perseus) — i.e. FDM particle mass upper limits m ≲ 1.1–1.5 × 10⁻²² eV; above these values FDM effects are too weak to solve the GC timing problem.
3. **Bonus:** FDM naturally alleviates the classic Fornax GC timing problem (the 6-GC configuration).
