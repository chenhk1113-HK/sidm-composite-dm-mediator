## Abstract (from the PDF)

> We report the non-detection of stellar light associated with the dark galaxy candidate Cloud-9. Ultra-deep imaging obtained with HiPERCAM at the Gran Telescopio Canarias (GTC) reaches surfacebrightness limits of 31.4 and 31.0 mag/arcsec2 in g and r, respectively, approximately ten times deeper than previous deep imaging of the region. No stellar emission is detected within the central 1 1 (1.31.3 kpc) region of Cloud-9. Assuming an old, metal-poor stellar population, these limits imply a stellar surface mass density of no more than 0.01 M/pc2 and an upper limit on the total stellar mass of 1.6104 M. This independent constraint from integrated light complements previous limits based on resolved-star counts and strengthens the case for Cloud-9 as a candidate starless galaxy.
