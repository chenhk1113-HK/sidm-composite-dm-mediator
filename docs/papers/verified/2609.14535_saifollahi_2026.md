## Key Findings

1. **Discovery:** Fornax-7, a faint stellar system (likely an ultra-faint dwarf satellite or remote star cluster) near the Fornax dSph in Euclid DR1 deep imaging + resolved-star photometry.
2. **Properties:** ~170 M_sun in stars, ~10.4 Gyr old, projected distance ~2.8 kpc from Fornax center, well inside Fornax's tidal radius → potential satellite-member system.
3. **Method:** forward modeling of CMDs against TRGB and RGB-tip luminosity functions; Fornax's own stellar-density-based mass map (§4) — a potential reconstruction without assuming an NFW form.
