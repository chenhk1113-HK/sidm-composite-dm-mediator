## Key Findings

1. **Model:** SASHIMI/SatGen semi-analytic CDM subhalo histories + the Yang+ parametric gravothermal model (their Eqs. 2.1-2.7, collapse parameter Υ(t) = ∫ dt/t_c with threshold Υ > 1.1), with the SAME velocity-dependent double power-law σ(v) family the project uses (their Eq. 2.5-2.6).
2. **Fiducial parameters:** σ0/m = 147.1 cm²/g and **w = 120 km/s adopted from Nadler, Yang & Yu 2023 (ApJL 958, L39)** — chosen there to produce core-collapsed subhalos for the SDSS J0946+1006 perturber while keeping large cores in isolated low-concentration halos.
3. **Simultaneous-perturber requirement (their Section 4.4):** reproducing ALL THREE project perturbers (PJ011646, SDSS J0946+1006, JVAS B1938+666) requires **w ≳ 10² km/s and σ0/m ≳ 10² cm²/g**. At w = 24 km/s the massive J0946 progenitors have internal velocities well above w, their interactions are velocity-suppressed, and they never collapse enough — J0946 cannot be reproduced.
4. **Per-perturber yields at the fiducial:** PJ011646 ~0.6 cored SIDM subhalos per host vs ~0.002 in CDM; SDSS J0946+1006 ~3 core-collapsed vs 0 in 900 CDM realizations; B1938+666 "detection V" ~220 core-collapsed + ~20 cored per host inside the 5σ posterior vs ~0.2 in CDM (the CDM candidates need median accretion redshift z ~ 13).
5. **σ0 = 147.1 coincidence:** the identical σ0 number also appears in Yang+ 2024 parametric ([43]) paired with **w = 24.33 km/s** (confirmed directly from our local [43] PDF, §"the simulation in ref [5] assumes σ0/m = 147.1 and w = 24.33"). Both pairings trace to the Nadler/Yang/Yu line of work; they are two calibrations of the same family, not one number.

## Notes
