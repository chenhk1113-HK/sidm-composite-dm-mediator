## Key findings

Independent evidence, from a different galaxy sample and group, that **massive galaxy halos contain less inner dark matter than predicted** — the same qualitative deficit as [55b], at the same velocity scale. The draft pairs the two because agreeing results from independent samples are stronger than either alone.
