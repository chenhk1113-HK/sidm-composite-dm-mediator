## Key Findings

1. **Method:** 36 FIRE-2 Milky Way analogs; a GD-1-like cluster stream perturbed by a **Sgr-like merger at t≈8 Gyr** (M_inf ≈ 10^11 M_sun, pericentric ~17 kpc); 10 streams per simulation; 43% of simulated MWs underwent a Sgr-like merger within the last 10 Gyr.
2. **Spur rates (§3, abstract):** spurs appear in **50%** of streams in the merger configuration, **25%** under the bar alone, **28%** from subhalo populations.
3. **Morphological degeneracy (§3.1):** merger spurs are nearly indistinguishable from subhalo spurs in morphology AND velocity signature; merger scenarios produce both spurs and gaps simultaneously — exactly the GD-1 look.
4. **Mechanism:** spurs arise from a **resonant phase wrap-up** triggered when the bar's pattern speed matches the stream orbital frequency (bar-saturating regime); the phase pattern alone can identify origin.
5. **Prediction:** Gaia DR4 / LSST should see more spur-rich streams around the MW than CDM-only expectations.
