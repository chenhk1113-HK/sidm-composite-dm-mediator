## Caveats

- Baryons complicate the picture: "the addition of baryons in the FP runs ... creat[es] different trends in vSIDM and SIDM1", and adiabatic contraction in baryon-dominated regions counteracts self-interactions, so large cores no longer form there.
- The concentration statement is tied to the **Einasto** parametrisation. Whether the SIDM concentration offset survives in a Y24-type fit is a separate result in the same paper, and any use of this paper as "SIDM lowers concentration" must say which fit family produced the number.

## Verdict for the project

**methodology-caveat, and partially contests.** The project's headline Cloud-9 claim (NFW fit 7σ below the c–M relation; SIDM reduces it to 3σ) is a statement about fitted concentrations. This paper shows that for SIDM halos the fitted concentration depends on the profile family used, and that SIDM halos fitted with Einasto sit below CDM at *all* masses. Any v19.2-F statement of the form "SIDM repairs the concentration deficit" should be checked against a Y24-style fit, not only against an NFW fit.
