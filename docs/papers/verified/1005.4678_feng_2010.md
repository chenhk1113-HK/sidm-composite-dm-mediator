## Key findings

Determines how large indirect-detection signals can be under Sommerfeld-enhanced annihilation while keeping the correct thermal relic density. Refines earlier work with a detailed treatment of **resonant Sommerfeld enhancement** and of the effect of Sommerfeld enhancement **on freeze-out**. Four effects matter: the cutoff of resonant enhancement, the equilibration of force carriers, the kinetic-decoupling temperature, and the efficiency of self-interactions at preserving thermal velocity distributions.

A striking result: for resonantly-enhanced Sommerfeld annihilation, DM freezes out but may then **chemically recouple**, implying highly suppressed indirect signals — contrary to naive expectation. This is the paper the draft relies on for the **Yukawa-suppression** mechanism and its Born-limit behaviour.
