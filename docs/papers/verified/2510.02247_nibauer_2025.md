## Key Findings

1. **Data:** Gaia DR3 + DESI legacy survey GD-1 positions/radial velocities; three radial-dispersion bins — 1.7±1.2, 4.8±1.7, 3.7±1.3 km/s (§2, Fig. 1). The middle bin containing the spur is ~4σ higher than smooth-MW models.
2. **Method:** simulation-based ABC inference over Model I (subhalo population: f_sub, subhalo mass function, mass-size relation) and Model II (dark-matère subhalo inner-slope variants, c_-2), including LMC host + progenitor stream, baryonic bar/shapes, and 4625 particles/host from Bolshoi-Planck + Via Lactea II.
3. **Subhalo abundance:** f_sub = 0.05 (+0.121/−0.083) at 68% (§4-§6, Fig. 5) — consistent with ΛCDM expectations (Moster 2022); no need to invoke beyond-ΛCDM physics.
4. **Compactness:** preferred smaller subhalos, r_s/r_s,CDM = 0.3±0.23 at f_sub=10% (§5.1, §6) — tension with ΛCDM mass-size relation but stated as "consistent with expectations"; degeneracy with f_sub explicitly acknowledged (Fig. 5).
5. **WDM:** half-mode mass log10(M_hm/M_sun) < 8.6 at 95% (§5.3) — competitive warm/fuzzy-DM bound.
6. **Model II (SIDM-flavored steep subhalos):** c_-2 > [422,190,90] kpc⁻¹ at M_200 = [10⁵,10⁶,10⁷] M_sun, vs CDM [50,43,37] (§6, Fig. 8) — steep-concentration subhalos remain viable explanations of the density enhancements.
