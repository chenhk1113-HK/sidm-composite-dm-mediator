## Key findings

Detailed fits to DM halos from dwarf galaxies to galaxy clusters show that **SIDM can consistently solve the DM-deficit problem across all scales** even though individual systems show wide diversity in halo properties. Because the characteristic DM velocity differs between systems, the fit *measures* the self-interaction cross section as a function of kinetic energy and so infers SIDM particle-physics parameters.

The headline result is a **mildly velocity-dependent** cross section: ~2 cm^2/g on galaxy scales falling to ~0.1 cm^2/g on cluster scales. This is the founding "one model, many scales" result for velocity-dependent SIDM.
