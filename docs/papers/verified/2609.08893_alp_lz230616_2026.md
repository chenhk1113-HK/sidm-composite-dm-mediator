# ALP-mediated inelastic dark matter and the LUX-ZEPLIN high-recoil candidate event LZ230616

- **Authors:** Guan-Wen Yuan, Bo Zhang, Wen-Yu Cao, Lei Feng, Ruizhi Yang
- **Published:** 2026-09-08 (7 pages)
- **Local PDF:** `docs/papers/verified/2609.08893_alp_lz230616_2026.pdf`

## Abstract (from the PDF)

> The LUX-ZEPLIN (LZ) Collaboration has reported a high-energy candidate
> event LZ230616 with a reconstructed nuclear recoil energy
> E_R = 248 +/- 23_stat +/- 23_sys keV. We investigate a possible
> interpretation in terms of inelastic scattering between two Majorana
> dark matter states mediated by an axionlike particle coupled to
> gluons. The positive mass splitting suppresses low-energy recoils,
> while the momentum dependence of the interaction reshapes the
> high-energy spectrum. We treat the dark-sector and gluonic couplings
> independently and retain the momentum-dependent nucleon form factors
> and xenon nuclear responses. Using an approximate single-event
> likelihood, we find that, for m_a = 0.3 GeV, a narrow spectrum near
> the candidate energy arises at m_chi ~ 0.35 TeV and delta ~ 330 keV,
> although this configuration requires a large coupling product and is
> highly sensitive to the Galactic halo speed cutoff.

## Key numbers extracted from the PDF

- Model: L_int = -(g_chi a) psi2bar i gamma5 psi1 + (g_G / 8) a G^A_{mu nu} G~^{A mu nu}; g_chi, g_G dimension GeV^-1; two Majorana states with m_2 = m_1 + delta, delta > 0.
- Dominant nucleon operator O6; Wilson coefficient c6N(Q) = [4 (m_N^ref)^2 g_G a_N(-Q^2)] / (Q^2 + m_a^2) * g_chi, with pion/eta pole form factor (m_pi = 134.98 MeV, m_eta = 547.86 MeV), WimPyDD nuclear response; 129Xe (J=1/2, f=0.26401), 131Xe (J=3/2, f=0.21232).
- SHM: v0 = 238 km/s, v_esc = 544 km/s, rho = 0.3 GeV/cm^3; exposure 2.84 tonne yr; energy window 5.4-269.9 keV; energy kernel sigma_E = sqrt(23^2 + 23^2) = 32.53 keV.
- Table I (m_a = 0.3 GeV): best fit (m_chi, delta) = (0.35 TeV, 330 keV), q_LZ = 0, |g_G| = 0.21 GeV^-2; q_LZ <= 1: (1.25 TeV, 320 keV), 0.99, 2.30e-4 GeV^-2; q_LZ <= 2: (1.04 TeV, 225 keV), 1.99, 4.30e-5 GeV^-2; elastic axion (delta = 0): 5.0 TeV, 2.84, 1.42e-5 GeV^-2.
- Minimum speeds at E_R = 248 keV: ~814.5 km/s (129Xe), ~809.9 km/s (131Xe) at the best-fit point.
- Significance quoted from the LZ paper (arXiv:2609.02823): 3.4 local / 2.6 global; not derived here.

## Limit content

**None.** The paper publishes no direct-detection exclusion limit:
mechanical PDF-text scan returns 0 hits for "cm-2", "cm^2", "exclusion",
"upper limit", "90% CL", "95% CL". What it publishes is the coupling
product *required* to yield one expected event, and it states of its own
statistic: "neither reproduces the official LZ likelihood nor defines
statistically calibrated confidence regions", and "the coupling products
quoted in the main text ... do not include a relic-abundance prior or
collider and flavor constraints".
