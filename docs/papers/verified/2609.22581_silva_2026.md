## Key Findings

1. **Combination:** DESI DR1 1D Lyman-α power spectrum + Planck 2018 CMB + DESI DR2 BAO, validating the DESI two-parameter P1D compression for a different-temperature sterile neutrino (DTS) scenario (residual cosmological dependence < 0.15%).
2. **N_eff:** CDM+Neff gives **N_eff < 3.41 (95%)** from CMB+DESI-BAO+DESI-P1D — no significant evidence for extra radiation or a sterile component.
3. **Sterile mass:** DTS scenario gives **m_s^eff < 0.061 eV** (95%) — among the most restrictive upper bounds on this model; adding DESI-P1D improves the mass bound by ~7× over CMB alone.
4. **Thermal relics:** allowed N_eff mapped to decoupling-temperature lower bounds reaching the QCD epoch.
