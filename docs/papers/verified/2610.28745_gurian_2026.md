## Key Findings

1. **Purpose:** reconciles three gravothermal-collapse codes — SphDSMC (this paper), Perseus (Tavakoli et al.), and the Loeb/Yang semianalytic family used by arXiv:2305.16176 (project ref [43]) — for elastic hard-sphere SIDM halos.
2. **Knudsen criterion (§3):** regime boundary Kn = (1/n_cσ)(1/r_c)·(0.1054 (r_s/r_c)²)^(3/2); **LMFP when Kn>1, dense-core/SMFP when Kn<1**; the 10%-collapse time t_c/t_r tracks Kn monotonically.
3. **Yang-family validity:** the Loeb 150C scaling is accurate **only for LMFP, C>~0.5** (their Halo 1: Kn=6.65 at σ=250 cm²/g); at **σ≲100 cm²/g** halos enter dense-core/SMFP and the Yang family becomes inaccurate.
4. **SMFP scaling differs:** core-collapse time ∝ **σ²** in SMFP vs ∝ **1/σ** in LMFP — a qualitative flip the [43] family does not capture.
5. **Calibration constants (Eqs. 12, 17):** B = 3.91 (t_0.1/t_r, halo-independent); n_L = 179, A = 0.917, W = 2.23, X̂ = 0.052 (LMFP fit); core-collapse times 18-110 t_r across C=0.4-1.1.
6. **Merger effect (§5):** mergers delay collapse by up to ~18 t_r.
