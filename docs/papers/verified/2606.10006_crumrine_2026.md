## Key Findings

1. **Scope:** MIXED dark matter — only a fraction f (tested down to 50%) of DM is non-CDM; four scenarios: fuzzy DM (FDM), and interacting DM scattering with photons (γ-DM), neutrinos (ν-DM), or protons (p-DM).
2. **Method:** maps COZMIC II zoom-in (beyond-CDM initial conditions) bounds on the linear matter power spectrum — calibrated on DES + PS1 MW satellite abundances, halos ~10⁸ M_sun, k ≳ 10 h/Mpc — onto each scenario's parameter space; composite-envelope method for FDM (Fig. 2).
3. **Results:** new leading lower limits on FDM mass m(f) and lower limits on interaction rate σ_j for γ/ν/p-DM, weakening with decreasing f via power-law degeneracies; transfer functions plateau to (1−f)² at high mediator mass (Fig. 7).
4. **No SIDM self-interaction content** — the interactions here are DM–SM (photon/neutrino/proton), not DM–DM.
