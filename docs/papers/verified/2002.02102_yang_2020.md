## Caveats

- DF2/DF4 here are **tidal-history simulations of ultra-diffuse galaxies**, not Cloud-9 analogues; the velocity regime is group-scale, not dwarf-scale.
- The paper's "4σ" is a deviation from a c–M relation median with a scatter taken from its own reference [58] — a different scatter convention from the project's 0.16 dex (Diemer+ 2019). Do not compare 4σ and 7σ without stating both conventions.

## Verdict for the project

**corroborates the structure of the argument, not the numbers.** This is the strongest published precedent for the project's central move: a halo observed ~4σ **below** the concentration-mass relation is repaired by SIDM because core formation raises the fitted concentration and enhances tidal mass loss, whereas CDM needs an implausibly low concentration. Cloud-9's 7σ → 3σ is the same argument at a different mass and velocity. It also documents the competing explanation (CDM + extreme low concentration), which the project should cite rather than leave unstated.
