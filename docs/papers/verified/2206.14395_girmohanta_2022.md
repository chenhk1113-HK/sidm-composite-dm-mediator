## Key findings

Studies an asymmetric DM model in which SIDM is a **Dirac fermion coupled to a scalar or vector mediator**, with χχ → χχ described by perturbation theory. Computes the total cross section σ, the transfer cross section σ_T, and the viscosity cross section σ_V.

The paper's central technical contribution — and the reason it is cited in this draft — is the **analytic and numerical comparison between results including BOTH t-channel and u-channel exchanges and the t-channel-only approximation** that has often been used in the literature. It shows the velocity dependence of these cross sections is in accord with observational data. For **identical particles** the u-channel contribution carries explicit **m_χ^4** terms and is not negligible.
