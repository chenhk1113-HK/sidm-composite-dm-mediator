## Key Findings

1. **Cloud-9 is a compact H I cloud in the vicinity of the spiral galaxy M94** (NOT NGC 1023 as previously noted in v19.2-F notes)
2. **Distance ~4.4 Mpc** (same recession velocity as M94)
3. **Compact (~1' radius, or ~1.4 kpc)**
4. **Dynamically cold (W50 = 12 km/s)**
5. **Non-rotating**
6. **H I mass ~10^6 M☉**
7. **Starless** (RELHIC — Reionization-Limited H I Cloud — candidate)
8. **First RELHIC candidate of any known compact H I cloud**
9. **Provides strong support for ΛCDM prediction** of gas-filled starless dark matter halos on sub-galactic mass scales
10. **Constrains the present-day threshold halo mass for galaxy formation** (M_crit ≈ 10^9.7 M☉)

## Cloud-9 morphology (KEY for v40.44)

- **Compact, non-rotating, dynamically cold** (W50 = 12 km/s)
- **No tidal features, no rotation, no disturbance**
- **RELHIC: gas in thermal equilibrium with UVB**
- **"Susceptible to ram-pressure stripping"** — unlikely to be near a massive galaxy
- **In GROUP environment** (M94 group)
