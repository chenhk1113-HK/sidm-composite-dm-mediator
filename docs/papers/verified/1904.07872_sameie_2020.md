## Key Findings

1. **σ/m = 3 cm²/g explains diverse rotation curves of field spiral galaxies**
2. **For σ/m > 5 cm²/g, subhalos can experience SIDM core collapse** — central density INCREASES, halo becomes CUSPIER
3. **Core collapse in tidal field** — Dracos's host could be in core-collapse phase for σ/m > 5 cm²/g
4. **Anti-correlation** between central densities and pericenters of bright MW dSphs (supports core-collapse scenario)
5. **Mechanism:** SIDM thermalization + MW tidal field → core collapse or core expansion depending on cross section, pericenter, initial halo concentration
