## Key Findings

1. **Data:** 29 quadruply imaged quasars (JWST lensed quasar DM survey); forward-modeling of flux-ratio anomalies maps SIDM models into core-collapse timespace (t6/8, t8/10).
2. **Favored dark sector:** m_DM = 20-80 GeV, **MeV-scale mediator**, Yukawa potential strength near 10^-3, giving **σ/m = 10^2-10^4 cm²/g at 10 km/s**. Data disfavor σ(10 km/s) < 10 cm²/g; σ < 5 cm²/g is indistinguishable from CDM (collapse time exceeds the age of the Universe in low-mass halos).
3. **Statistical strength:** CDM disfavored with **Bayes factors 8:1 to 24:1** depending on modeling assumptions (Table I assumption sets; 24:1 and 13:1 for the two likelihoods); CDM is NOT formally excluded.
4. **Minimum-collapse-time floor:** using the Gurian et al. gravothermal model, elastic scattering cannot drive field-halo collapse arbitrarily fast: t_c,min ≈ 10²(r_s/v_s), reached near σ_eff ≈ 6.8/sqrt(σ...) regime; likelihood is bimodal along LMFP and SMFP branches — same regime physics as van den Bosch & Tokayer 2026.
5. **Particle-physics packaging:** a **neutrinophilic scalar dilepton** decaying invisibly (no Higgs mixing) evades direct/indirect bounds and the thermal-history requirement T_kd ≳ 7 keV (needed to keep the linear matter power spectrum CDM-like to k ≈ 200 Mpc^-1). MW satellite evaporation and cluster upper limits cut off the high-σ region.
