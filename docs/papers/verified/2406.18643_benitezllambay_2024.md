## Key findings

VLA D-array interferometric observations of **Cloud-9** — the gas-rich, starless DM halo candidate near M94. The observations reveal it as a **dynamically cold** (W50 ≈ 12 km/s), **non-rotating**, spatially asymmetric system with gas compression on one side and a tail-like structure on the other, features likely produced by **ram pressure**. The authors conclude Cloud-9 is consistent with a gas-rich (M_HI ~ 10^6 M_sun), starless DM halo of mass M200 ~ 5 × 10^9 M_sun.
