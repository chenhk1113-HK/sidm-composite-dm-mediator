## Key Findings

1. **Method:** SIDM1 effective-Boltzmann implementation (collision term C1 with velocity scaling σ/m = σ0 (1000 km/s / v)^4) fit to DESI DR2 BAO + DESI DR1 Lyα + Pantheon+ SN + CMB distance priors.
2. **Key numbers:** **lnB = 5.57 (DESI BAO alone) and 8.72 (DESI+CMB)** for SIDM1 over CDM — a >3σ-strength preference for interacting DM at the background level; best-fit σ/m at z=0 **0.078** vs Planck2018-era 0.17 (looser than previously claimed).
3. **Upper limits:** σ/m < 0.2 cm²/g (DESI BAO alone); **< 0.01 cm²/g (with CMB priors)** at v_rel ≈ 1000 km/s.
4. Lyα keeps sensitivity to z≈1.1.

## Notes

- **Model-mismatch caveat:** SIDM1 effective collision term ≠ our resonant Sommerfeld cross section; their "σ(m)(v)" velocity scaling (v^-4) differs from our double power law.
- Their σ0/m ~ 1.47 cm²/g normalization (from Arkani-Hamed+2018 SIDM1) is unrelated to our 147.1.
