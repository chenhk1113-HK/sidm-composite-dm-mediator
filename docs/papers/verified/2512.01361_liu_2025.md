## Key Findings

1. **Method:** fuzzy-DM (FDM) simulation (ULyMA) + GD-1 stream proper-motion dispersion σ_los = 0.0316 mas/yr (Banik et al. 2025, DESI DR1) compared against CDM N-body.
2. **WDM bound:** m_WDM > 6.20 keV at 95% (§5.2) — thermal relic; mass function suppression M_peak < 10⁷·(5 keV/m_WDM)^1.4 M_sun.
3. **FDM bound:** m_FDM > 1.74×10⁻²⁰ eV (§6.3) — de Broglie scale k_d ≈ 4 h/Mpc at that mass; 2× weaker than the WDM bound.
4. **Substructure census:** only 7-8 subhalos >10⁷ M_sun within 50 kpc across all models; ~2000 subhalos <10⁶ M_sun in CDM; a single perturber ≥4.7×10⁸ M_sun (80 kpc from GD-1) reproduces the observed dispersion.
5. **Authors' own claim:** FDM disfavored as solution to small-scale problems because cored dwarfs are hard to retain (§discussion).
