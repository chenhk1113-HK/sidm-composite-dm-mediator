# Horigome+ 2025 — Stringent Constraints on SIDM from MW Satellites

**Authors:** S. Ando, S. Horigome, et al.
**arXiv:** 2503.13650
**Submitted:** 17 March 2025
**Status:** VERIFIED (PDF downloaded 2026-10-17, v40.24)

## Abstract (verbatim from arXiv)

> We present stringent constraints on the self-interacting dark matter (SIDM) cross section using the kinematics of Milky Way satellite galaxies. By combining the astrometric and spectroscopic data from Gaia DR3 with N-body simulations of subhalo evolution, we determine the upper limits on the cross section per unit mass σ/m. Assuming a log-uniform prior on σ0/m over [10⁻³, 10³] cm²/g, we obtain 95%-percentile upper limits of 0.8 cm²/g for w=10 km/s and 0.04 cm²/g for the velocity-independent case. The combined analysis decisively prefers CDM to SIDM when the self-interaction cross section per unit mass, σ/m, exceeds ∼0.2 cm²/g, if a [specific form is used].
