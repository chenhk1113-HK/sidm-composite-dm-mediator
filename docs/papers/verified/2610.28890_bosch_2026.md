## Key Findings

1. **New calibration standard:** a spherical Direct Simulation Monte Carlo code (SphDSMC) without force softening, reaching far higher central densities than N-body, is used to recalibrate the gravothermal-fluid (GTF) conductivity. N-body converges to SphDSMC only for softening lengths MUCH smaller than normally adopted.
2. **The harmonic LMFP<->SMFP interpolation is inaccurate.** A gentler transition with a single LMFP conductivity parameter fits NFW halos over a range of concentrations/truncations with (α, β) = (0.5, 0.9); for their alternative "Core" profile (0.57, 0.94). Percent-level accuracy requires re-calibration per density profile. Different published calibration constants disagree by >25% — plausibly a softening/systematics artifact, not physics.
3. **Revised LMFP collapse time is somewhat SHORTER than previous estimates, BUT collapse slows dramatically once the core becomes optically thick (SMFP).** Any collapsing core becomes optically thick before it becomes relativistic; self-similar LMFP collapse-time formulae (their Eq. 32 — the family of [43] eq. 2.2) can SEVERELY underestimate the time to reach extreme densities.
4. **They explicitly recompute a Yang-style case** (their α = 1.0, C = 0.75 — the same constants our `gravothermal_yang2024.py` implements; NFW with ρ_s = 0.019 M_sun/pc³, r_s = 2.6 kpc, V_s = 83.6 km/s, σ/m = 5 cm²/g): super-exponential LMFP collapse initially, then the slowdown once optically thick.
5. **Black-hole seeds:** SIDM gravothermal collapse has generally been OVERESTIMATED in speed; seed-BH formation times are orders of magnitude longer than previously assumed — unlikely to explain massive JWST high-z BHs without extra physics.
6. **Reliability floor:** reliable collapse times need ≥10^6 particles per halo even in SphDSMC; they calibrate on t_3 (time for core density to reach 10³ ρ_s) because at high σ there is no well-defined "collapse time".

## How v19.2-F uses this paper

- **Adjudication (2026-11-03): ADOPT as the standing calibration caveat for the [43] t_c chain.** Our dSph-regime cases (σ_eff ≈ 7 cm²/g, BM2/Cosmo-501 — κ_m in their LMFP-like 0.05-0.16 zone) stay near the valid regime; the new calibration is where the recorded BM2-vs-Cosmo-501 10.7x spread plausibly comes from.
- **Open item to check in a dedicated re-run:** high-σ Cloud-9-type states (σ ~ 174 cm²/g near 29 km/s) push systems optically thick EARLY; if so, producing RELHIC-level central densities within the available time could be slower than the parametric formula predicts. NOT yet quantified against our numbers — do not quote any t_core for high-σ systems without this check.
- Confirmed live 2026-11-03: the project paper has **no black-hole-seed claims** (grep empty) — no existing claim is killed.
