## Key Findings

1. **Claim:** Gaia DR3 proper motions reveal a diffuse "cocoon" of stars heated by repeated GD-1 stream/subhalo interactions, surrounding the stream.
2. **Method:** mock GD-1 in m000 (Giggle-Noodle) MW (N24 halo) with 108k particles (Copernicus); model A = all subhalos inside stream volume (5678), model B = only 585 subhalos that directly hit the stream; full stream-particle bookkeeping of energy/velocity injections.
3. **Predicted cocoon (§3.3, Fig. 4):** 2400 heated stars; proper-motion dispersion **σ_v = 2.49 mas/yr** (smooth stream) vs **6.13 mas/yr** (cocoon region) at φ=75°; the 2025 detection (Banik et al. 2025) lies in the model-B 50th-percentile prediction band.
4. **Subhalo census:** N24 = 743 satellites inside 100 kpc, 445 inside 50 kpc, 151 inside 25 kpc; 47% of mass in the two largest subhalos.
5. **CDM consistency:** cocoon properties match ΛCDM expectations from AbacusMW2; "cocoon is a generic feature of GD-1 in CDM" (Fig. 5).
