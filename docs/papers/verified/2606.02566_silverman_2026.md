## Key Findings

1. **6 DM-only zoom-in ~10^10 M☉ halos with σ/m = 70 cm²/g (s70)** + 1 m10b run with σ/m = 30 cm²/g (s30)
2. **100% collapse fraction among QUIESCENT halos (3/3):** m10q, m10d, m10i (all with quiescent merger histories)
3. **0% collapse fraction among SUSTAINED halos (0/3):** m10b, m10f, m10j (all with sustained mergers)
4. **Merger-induced heat transport drives non-collapsing halos to central densities well BELOW the gravothermal fluid model prediction**
5. **Quantitative result:** 1:3 merger at 7.7 Gyr caused m10b's ρ_c to drop by factor 8.7 (s70) or 3.9 (s30)
6. **Mechanism:** Mergers inject orbital kinetic energy → inward heat flow → core expansion → collapse prevention
7. **Halo-to-halo variation NOT captured by the fluid model** — merger history is essential

## Diagnostics for Predicting Collapse (from §3.3)

- **Merger mass ratio:** M_sub/M_host ≥ 0.1 (massive mergers matter)
- **Orbital radiality:** vtan/vtot ≤ 0.2 (radial mergers matter more)
- **Quiescence time interval:** max time between mergers
- **Time of merger:** late mergers (after ~5 Gyr) more disruptive
