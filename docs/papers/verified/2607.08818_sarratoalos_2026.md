## Caveats

- Reports slopes at a **fixed radius**, not at the density peak — the same convention family as the project's "slope at fixed fractional radius" (see registry §1, Zhao+ 2025 convention row). Comparing a -1.5-vs-0.1 slope contrast from this paper against a peak-based slope from another paper is invalid without converting conventions.
- Quotes no σ/m bound: it is a profile-structure comparison between dwarf simulations and observations, not a SIDM constraint.
- No volume/page yet — cite as "accepted, arXiv:2607.08818" until Crossref registers it.

## Verdict for the project

**background-only, with one usable number.** The 2-3σ NFW central-density over-prediction for cored dwarfs is the cleanest published statement in this intake that "an NFW fit to a cored dwarf is biased", which is the mechanism behind the project's Cloud-9 NFW-vs-SIDM comparison.
