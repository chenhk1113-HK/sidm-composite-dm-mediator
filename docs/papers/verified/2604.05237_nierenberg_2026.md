## Key Findings

1. **Sample:** 61 strong-lensing galaxy groups/clusters with JWST NIRCam+F277W resolved arcs — a ~7× sample increase over prior low-mass subhalo lenses.
2. **Lowest-mass subhalo detections (§4.2):** three subhalos with halo mass ≈ 10^8.2, 10^8.3, 10^8.6 M_sun (95% lower limits reach 10^7.7–10^9.7); c200 ~ 10^9.7–10^10 M_sun⁻¹ kpc — highest concentrations yet from lensing at these masses.
3. **Abundance (§5.1):** ~0.32 subhalos per 10^10 M_sun of cluster mass (61 subhalos total); subhalos trace light (galaxies) down to M_sub/M_host ~10⁻⁵ (Fig. 12).
4. **Comparison to CDM (§5.2, Fig. 13):** measurements lie within predictions of El-Badry+2022 CDM extrapolation; consistent with observed Local Group / Fornax satellite mass ranges.

## How v19.2-F uses this paper

- **Adjudication (2026-11-03): CORROBORATES (weak) — pushes the observed subhalo census toward 10⁸ M_sun within CDM expectations.** No SIDM test, no σ constraint; useful as the empirical floor of the halo population our SIDM subhalo-suppression predictions (SIDM Concerto 50% suppression, Sameie+ channel) must be compared against at low mass.
- Supports the reality and detectability of 10⁸ M_sun subhalos — the mass scale where fuzzy/WDM suppression and SIDM suppression predictions diverge most.
