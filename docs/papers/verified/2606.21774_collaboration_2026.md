## Key Findings

1. **New channel:** stellar tidal streams in 13 external galaxies (11 confirmed + 2 candidates, at ~50-120 Mpc) map the **shape and center** of host dark halos out to several stream radii — a sub-virial halo-geometry probe distinct from lensing or satellite kinematics.
2. **Result:** projected axis ratios cluster near **q_proj = 0.95 ± 0.05** averaged over hosts (§4-5) — halos are broadly spherical/oblate at stream radii, mildly flattened; a non-spherical population is not excluded.
3. **Streams trace the potential:** stream orbits are consistent with the host halo potential extending beyond the visible range; halo centers recoverable to within ~1 kpc for the best systems.
4. **No SIDM test performed:** the paper reports geometry only; no cross-section constraint, no gravothermal modeling.
