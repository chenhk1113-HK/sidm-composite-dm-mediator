## Abstract (from the PDF)

> Context. Ultra-faint dwarf (UFD) galaxies are a promising probe for dark matter (DM) physics as they are the most DM-dominated systems known. The Milky Way (MW) hosts many UFDs for which the properties of their DM distribution have been inferred from measurements of their stellar kinematics. If DM has self-interactions beyond gravity, the UFD halos may undergo a gravothermal evolution, giving rise to a population of galaxies with more diverse DM density profiles. Aims.
