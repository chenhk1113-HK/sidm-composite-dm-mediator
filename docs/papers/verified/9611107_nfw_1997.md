## Why this entry exists

v19.2-F uses the NFW profile as the observable being tested on Cloud-9 — "NFW" appears 49 times in `PAPER_V1_DRAFT.md` and `r_s`/`ρ_s`/`c_200` are computed from M_200 and c_200 in `scripts/` — yet the paper's 43-entry reference list contains **no NFW reference at all**. This is the primary citation gap this intake closes.

## Convention note (matters for this project)

This paper defines masses and densities relative to the **critical density** ρ_crit = 3H^2/8πG. v19.2-F uses ρ_crit = 1.381×10^-7 M_sun/pc^3 at H_0 = 67.66 with Planck-2018 flat ΛCDM, and c ≡ r_200/r_s. **Cite this 1997 paper for the profile and concentration convention, not the 1996 one** (whose quoted quantities assume H_0 = 50 — see the companion entry).

## Caveats

- The paper's own c–M statement is qualitative (c decreases with mass); the quantitative c(M) relation the project uses comes from Diemer+ 2019 (already in the KB), not from this paper. Do not attribute a c–M scatter or normalisation to NFW 1997.
