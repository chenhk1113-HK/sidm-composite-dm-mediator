## Key Findings

1. **Reframe:** the GD-1 velocity-dispersion excess can be read as a **primordial small-scale power spectrum** measurement rather than a subhalo census — a stream crossing regions of different primordial power is mathematically equivalent to a population of unseen minihalos.
2. **Method:** emulator over ΛCDM + log-spaced bins of small-scale power (k up to 84.6 h/Mpc), calibrated on Aquila/Dark-ESPRESSO subhalos; Gaussian-process surrogate; DESI Y1 1D Lyα combined via joint likelihood.
3. **Isocurvature:** uncorrelated isocurvature fraction A_S² < 0.379 (95%) — strongest small-scale isocurvature bound from this channel.
4. **N_eff:** 3.02 ± 0.10 (GD-1 + Lyα, Planck baseline).
5. **Neutrino mass:** Σm_ν < 0.072 eV (GD-1 + CMB + Lyα) — competitive but model-dependent at k ≳ 10 h/Mpc.
6. **No SIDM content.**
