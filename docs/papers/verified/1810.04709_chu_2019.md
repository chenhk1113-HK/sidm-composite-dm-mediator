## Key findings

Uses a **model-independent** approach to show that **resonant SIDM (RSIDM)** naturally produces a velocity-dependent cross section of the kind the data suggest — cored profiles on small scales and a weaker cross section in clusters. Critically, it shows this does **not** require a light mediator, contrary to what is often assumed. Explicit realisations are given together with the astrophysical constraints.
