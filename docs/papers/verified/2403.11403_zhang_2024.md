## Caveats

- Its σ/m band (0.3–13 cm^2/g) brackets the project's low-velocity amplitude from **below** by 37–1600×; it is a group/cluster-orbit tidal simulation, not a dwarf-collision kinematic bound, so it is not a direct constraint on the project — it is a *comparison point* for the same velocity decade.
- Unrefereed. If cited, label it as a preprint.

## Verdict for the project

**contests the amplitude range, weakly.** Together with Yang+ 2020 (3–5 cm^2/g), it shows the DF2 literature sits at 0.3–13 cm^2/g while the project's low-velocity σ/m sits at 483 cm^2/g. That is worth stating in the paper as a literature-comparison sentence, not as a constraint.
