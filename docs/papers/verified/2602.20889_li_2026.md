## Key Findings

1. **Method:** anisotropic spherical JE solver with MUSE stellar kinematics for 20 massive ellipticals (incl. the strong lens J0946+1006); MGE + Sérsic photometry; three DM profiles (NFW, cored NFW, Einasto).
2. **Main result:** dark halos are consistent with **NFW / mildly steep inner slopes**: average α ≈ 0.94 (Table 3); 7/20 at 1σ, 14/20 at 2σ with α=1.
3. **J0946+1006 specifically (§4.3):** α = 1.04 ± 0.10, **M200 = 1.11×10¹³ M_sun**, c200 = 7.5 (+7.3/−3.4), consistent with Dutton+2019 ΛCDM concentration-mass. This **revises Sonnenfeld+ 2012's α≈1.7 claim** — that steep value came from the extreme 168″ arc (Fig. 10) and disappears when the arc is excluded.
4. **Concentration scatter:** c200 spans 0.421–18.25 (10th-90th percentile; Fig. 11); the authors link the large scatter partly to **dynamical heating from mergers**.

## How v19.2-F uses this paper

- **Adjudication (2026-11-03): MIXED — corroborates steep/NFW main halos at giant-elliptical scales; a REFINEMENT to the J0946+1006 evidence base that Tekverk+ 2026 (KB 2610.31881) leans on.** The J0946 main-halo super-NFW slope (α≈1.7) is NOT confirmed by independent stellar dynamics — when citing the J0946 system, cite α≈1.04±0.10 for the main halo; subhalo/lensing-arc claims remain separate.
- Merger-induced c200 scatter supports our stage-3 pivot to simulated merger trees (hand-classified merger histories are exactly the kind of approximation this scatter warns about).
- No SIDM content; NFW-consistency results are the CDM-side baseline our Model II/III steep alternatives must be compared against.
