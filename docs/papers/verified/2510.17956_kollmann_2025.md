## Key Findings

1. **Data:** Gemini "Gems of HFF" — strong-lensing arc in MACS J0416 with TWO independent image systems (galaxy-scale image 2.1 + cluster-scale arc a1) allowing independent measurement of the same subhalo.
2. **Subhalo detections (§3.4):** Subhalo 2: c200 = 5.4 (+2.3/−1.6) ×10⁸ M_sun⁻¹ kpc at M200 ≈ 10^5.9; Subhalo 3: c200 = 6.0 (+6.0/−4.2) ×10⁹ at M200 ≈ 10^7.4 (68%) — high concentrations persist across both lenses.
3. **Inner slopes (§4.1):** α = 2.24 (+0.36/−0.71) for subhalo 2; 2.10 (+0.25/−0.33) for subhalo 3, when allowed free — steep (NFW-like or steeper) is supported in both image systems.
4. **Robustness:** subhalo 3 recovered in all four public HFF cluster models and in both source galaxies (Fig. 9); c200(M200) follows expected CDM concentration-mass relation.
5. **Caveats:** free α + 2 sources → 14 free parameters; posterior priors influence inner-slope measurement; only ~2% of galaxy-group satellites analyzed.

## How v19.2-F uses this paper

- **Adjudication (2026-11-03): CORROBORATES the compact/steep-subhalos side of the observational picture; NO σ measurement.** Steep inner slopes (α≈2.1-2.2) in 10⁶-10⁷ M_sun subhalos are exactly the morphology our Model II-style steep subhalos and core-collapsed steep profiles predict — but this paper is done in a CDM framework and does not test SIDM.
- Cited as evidence that observed subhalo inner profiles can be steeper than cored, supporting our streams/lensing substructure posture (paired with Kollmann 2023 and the Li+ 2026 main-halo NFW result — steepness appears at subhalo scale, not necessarily main-halo scale).
