## Claim (as summarised from the abstract / secondary lookup — NOT yet full-text verified)

Galaxy collisions with **bursty star-formation feedback** can strip dark matter and produce
dark-matter-deficient galaxies. Two channels are distinguished by **baryonic binding energy**:

- bursty feedback **lowers the baryonic |E_bind| by ~15%**, producing *fewer but heavier*
  stripped galaxies;
- **elastic SIDM (sigma/m = 20 cm^2 g^-1)** can produce comparable dark-matter-deficient
  cores **without lowering |E_bind|**.

So |E_bind| is a *discriminator* between "this UDG is dark-matter-poor because of feedback"
and "because of dark-matter physics".

## v19.2-F relevance

- **Diagnostic idea for Channel 11** (DM-free UDGs: NGC 1052-DF2/DF4, DF9, FCC 224/240 —
  arXiv:1803.10237, 1901.05973, 2205.08552, 2502.05405, 2603.15860, 2605.24099). Channel 11
