## Numbers actually read from the PDF (Table 2, p. 17–18)

| Quantity | In 10⁻² M☉ pc⁻³ | In GeV cm⁻³ | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|
| ρ_total at the Solar location | 6.17 ± 0.20 | 2.34 ± 0.08 | MAF-estimated total mass density |
| ρ_b at the Solar location | 5.34 ± 0.42 | 2.03 ± 0.16 | baryons (Appendix A model) subtracted component |
| **ρ_DM at the Solar location (direct, unaveraged)** | 0.83 ± 0.47 | **0.32 ± 0.18** | point estimate at the Sun, no symmetry averaging |
| **ρ_DM(r = r̄) averaged over 15 independent points at the Solar radius** | 1.23 ± 0.13 | **0.47 ± 0.05** | the headline number, obtained **after imposing spherical symmetry** |

- The paper's own consistency check on the 15 points: χ² = 1.38, i.e. consistent with a constant value.
- **Unit cross-check performed by this repo (2026-11-17, not by the authors):** 1 M☉ pc⁻³ = 38.01 GeV cm⁻³. 1.23×10⁻² × 38.01 = 0.4675 ≈ 0.47 ✓; 0.83×10⁻² × 38.01 = 0.3155 ≈ 0.32 ✓. The two columns are mutually consistent.

## Conditions attached to the 0.47 ± 0.05 number

Verbatim from the abstract: *"Assuming spherical symmetry and averaging mass density measurements, we find a local dark matter density of 0.47 ± 0.05 GeV/cm³."* Verbatim from §1: *"…we find a dark matter density of 0.47 ± 0.05 GeV/cm³ at the Sun's distance from the Galactic Center."*

1. **Spherical symmetry is imposed** to average measurements at different locations — the authors state their method itself makes *no* symmetry assumption, and that the averaging (and hence the ±0.05) depends on adding one.
2. **Equilibrium is assumed** (steady collisionless Boltzmann equation); they test it and report consistency at the 10% level, "limited by the current precision of the normalizing flows".
3. **Baryons subtracted** using their own Appendix A model — the DM number inherits that model's error (note ρ_b carries ±0.42 in 10⁻² M☉ pc⁻³, larger than ρ_DM itself).
4. The authors' own caveat, twice: *"Although the uncertainties are large"* and *"systematic errors on our MAF-derived accelerations are too large to draw confident conclusions"* (§ near Fig. 13).
5. Volume analysed: stars within ~3 kpc of the Sun (4 kpc kinematic sample).

## What this paper does NOT provide

- **No SIDM content.** Text search of the downloaded PDF (2026-11-17, `grep -aic`): `self interacting` 0, `self interaction` 0, `SIDM` 0. It constrains the mass density of the Milky Way under collisionless equilibrium; it says nothing about σ/m and cannot be used as a SIDM constraint or as a cross-section prior.
- **No stellar streams.** `stream` occurs 0 times in the PDF text. Any claim that this is a "stellar stream" analysis is a citation error.
- **No competition/competition-derived data.** The method is unsupervised density estimation (normalizing flows) fit to Gaia DR3, not a supervised model trained on any Kaggle dataset. The lineage claimed in `kaggle.docx` L20–24 (2011–12 Kaggle baselines → this paper) is not supported by the paper.
