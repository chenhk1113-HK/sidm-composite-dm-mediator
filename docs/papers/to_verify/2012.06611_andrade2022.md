## Key result (from arXiv abstract)
> "We analyse strongly lensed images in eight galaxy clusters to measure their dark matter density profiles in the radial region between 10 kpc and 150 kpc. Combining all eight clusters, the upper limit on the cross section is 0.13 cm²/g at the 95% C.L. with the mean ⟨v_rel⟩ of about 1500 km/s."

So the actual bound is **σ/m < 0.13 cm²/g at ⟨v_rel⟩ ~ 1500 km/s**.
