## Key claim
Combines the semi-analytical SASHIMI structure formation model (predicts CDM subhalos) with a **parametric model** that maps CDM → SIDM halos. Result: SIDM subhalo populations down to sub-galactic mass scales for arbitrary input cross section, computed in minutes.
