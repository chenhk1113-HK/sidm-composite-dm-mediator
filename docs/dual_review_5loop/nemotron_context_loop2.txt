   10|

   92| -**σ/m**is the *microphysical* momentum-transfer cross-section per unit mass between two dark-matter particles. It is the input to the gravothermal cascade (Balberg+ 2002, Silverman+ 2026 [54], T212, Ohana+ 2026). At dSph/UFD velocities, σ/m from the paper's convention (Gaussian w=4.4 km/s, peak at v_target=29.4 km/s per Phase 44 free fit) reaches σ/m(15) ≈ 2.85 cm²/g at Fornax scale (background 0.052×(100/15)^1.93 = 2.04 + Gaussian tail 174×exp(-(15-29.4)²/(2×4.4²)) = 0.81; canonical Gaussian form per §3.6 canonical table).
